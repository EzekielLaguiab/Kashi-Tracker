Welcome to Kashi Tracker!
Track your income, expenses, and budgets all in one place. Let's get you set up in a few quick steps.
Set up your accounts
Add your bank accounts, e-wallets, and cash on hand. Kashi Tracker will track the balance of each one automatically.
Log your transactions

Set a budget & stay on track
Create monthly spending limits per category. Kashi Tracker shows your progress so you know exactly where you stand.
